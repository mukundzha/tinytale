## TinyTale

<p>tinyTale is a small language model experiment where i'm trying to train a tiny GPT-style transformer from scratch using public-domain fairy tales and folk stories. the goal is to seehow a small model learns storytelling patterns, characters, sentence structures, and those classic fairy-tale endings. Nothing too fancy, just experimenting with PyTorch, tokenization, and the basics of how language models actually work. </p>
